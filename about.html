About

The practitioner-educator behind the programmes.

Orakwe John, MBA is an AI Programs Lead and Global AI Advisor based in Lagos, Nigeria.

From awareness to transformation.

He designs and delivers executive AI upskilling and adoption programmes for central banks, ministries, banks and accelerators across Europe, the Middle East, Sub-Saharan Africa and Southeast Asia.

His method guides organisations from Level 1 (Awareness) to Level 5 (Transformative AI) on an AI maturity model. His consistent thesis: the tools were never the hard part, redesigning the work was.

He is faculty at Tekedia Institute, a startup advisor to accelerator programmes in the Gulf, and a daily voice on AI at work for leaders who need practical answers, not hype.
